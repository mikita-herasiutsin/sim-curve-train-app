| D-14 | 2026-10-06 | Preset sourcing | Presets are generated by a developer-only Python pipeline from `.ibt` files and the Garage 61 API (Pro needed for telemetry). Only derived, aggregated curves are shipped. | — |
| D-15 | 2026-10-06 | Out of MVP | Online leaderboard, career mode, preset sharing, wheel and trail-braking combo drills | — |
| D-16 | 2026-10-06 | Code signing | Releases are **unsigned** for now. The SmartScreen warning is documented in the README and the [releasing guide](../releasing.md). Signing is revisited in SCT-076. Answers Q-08. | — |
| D-17 | 2026-10-07 | Tolerance band | The band starts forgiving and tightens with level: **±10% → ±7% → ±4%**. The loosest step is for complete novices. Answers Q-03. | — |
| D-18 | 2026-10-07 | Throttle drills | The main throttle drill is a **staged exit**: a fast stab to a car-specific threshold (40–80%; GT3 and lower-powered road cars allow the higher end), then a progressive ramp to 100% at a car-specific rate. Some cars use two fast steps before the ramp. It keeps the car rotating after trail braking: too slow understeers, too fast loses the rear. Built as a trace drill. Answers Q-01. | — |
| D-19 | 2026-10-07 | Warm-up | A **fixed routine per preset**, defined in the preset JSON. Adaptive warm-ups need attempt history and come later. Answers Q-05. | — |
| D-20 | 2026-10-07 | Audio defaults | Audio is **on by default** and **silent inside the band**. Outside the band short beeps repeat, faster the further the pedal is past the band edge (parking-sensor style; chosen 2026-10-09 after a listening test, see `docs/investigations/2026-10-08-error-sound.md`). There is no lock chime. The default volume is quiet. | — |
| D-21 | 2026-10-09 | Trace targets | From the first trace drill test: trace curves are **rounded** (monotone cubic between points, zero slope at the ends, never above or below the points' range). A sample counts as in the band if it is within the tolerance of **any target value within ±150 ms**, so ramps such as 0→50% get a wider band while steady pressure keeps ±tolerance; scoring and audio use the same band as the screen. Every countdown shows **GO one second before** the rep starts. Scoring measures time in band and the error without a lag shift, so the result card matches what the screen and audio showed; the estimated lag only feeds the timing score. | — |
| D-22 | 2026-10-09 | Brake budget | Road-car presets (GT3, MX-5, Caterham, LMP) get a **guaranteed share of brake drills**; oval presets (NASCAR) get a **guaranteed share of lift drills**. The extractor takes per-kind minimums (`--min-brake N`, `--min-lift N`); unused slots fall back to the normal ranking. Each preset's extraction command passes the flags for its car class. | — |
| D-23 | 2026-10-10 | Leaderboard levels | The leaderboard is kept at **all three levels**: per drill, per preset and per warm-up. Answers Q-02. | — |
| D-24 | 2026-10-10 | Gamification | **None in the MVP** beyond scores and personal bests. XP and levels are post-MVP (SCT-070). Answers Q-06. | — |
| D-25 | 2026-10-10 | Warm-up length | A warm-up's **estimated length is 3-5 minutes**, and each warm-up step has **its own rep count**, which may be more than the drill's normal `reps`. The estimate per step is the engine's set length (lead-in, then the reps with a 2 s rest between them); time spent on screen between drills is left out. Chosen because the bundled drills are short: the whole GT3 preset at 5 reps per drill runs about 2 minutes. | — |
| D-26 | 2026-10-10 | Warm-up score | The overall warm-up score is the **mean of the steps' set averages**; a skipped step counts as 0, so skipping weak drills can't raise a leaderboard score. A set that ends before all its reps (Skip during the set, pedals unplugged) counts as skipped. | — |
