| D-17 | 2026-10-07 | Tolerance band | The band starts forgiving and tightens with level: **±10% → ±7% → ±4%**. The loosest step is for complete novices. Answers Q-03. | — |
| D-18 | 2026-10-07 | Throttle drills | The main throttle drill is a **staged exit**: a fast stab to a car-specific threshold (40–80%; GT3 and lower-powered road cars allow the higher end), then a progressive ramp to 100% at a car-specific rate. Some cars use two fast steps before the ramp. It keeps the car rotating after trail braking: too slow understeers, too fast loses the rear. Built as a trace drill. Answers Q-01. | — |
| D-19 | 2026-10-07 | Warm-up | A **fixed routine per preset**, defined in the preset JSON. Adaptive warm-ups need attempt history and come later. Answers Q-05. | — |
| D-20 | 2026-10-07 | Audio defaults | Audio is **on by default** and **silent inside the band**. Outside the band short beeps repeat, faster the further the pedal is past the band edge (parking-sensor style; chosen 2026-10-09 after a listening test, see `docs/investigations/2026-10-08-error-sound.md`). There is no lock chime. The default volume is quiet. | — |
| D-21 | 2026-10-09 | Trace targets | From the first trace drill test: trace curves are **rounded** (monotone cubic between points, zero slope at the ends, never above or below the points' range). A sample counts as in the band if it is within the tolerance of **any target value within ±150 ms**, so ramps such as 0→50% get a wider band while steady pressure keeps ±tolerance; scoring and audio use the same band as the screen. Every countdown shows **GO one second before** the rep starts. | — |
